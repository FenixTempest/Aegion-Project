## 💅 Styling Techniques Used

1. **CSS Grid** - Responsive feature card layouts
2. **Flexbox** - Flexible navigation and alignment
3. **Linear Gradients** - Background and text effects
4. **Radial Gradients** - Hover effects
5. **Pseudo-elements** - Animated underlines and effects
6. **Transform** - 3D effects and animations
7. **Filter** - Backdrop blur effects
8. **Box-shadow** - Depth and elevation
9. **CSS Variables** - Centralized theming
10. **Media Queries** - Responsive design
